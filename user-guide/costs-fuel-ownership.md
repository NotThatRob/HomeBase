# Costs, Fuel, and Ownership

HomeBase separates actual tracked spend from projected recurring obligations.
This makes totals easier to understand.

## Actual Spend

Actual spend is money already spent and logged in HomeBase.

Examples:

- Purchase price
- Service record costs
- Fuel logs

## Projected Recurring Costs

Recurring costs are ongoing obligations. They are useful for budgeting, but they
are not treated as already spent.

Examples:

- Insurance
- Registration
- Subscriptions
- Taxes
- Fees

## Recurring Costs

### Add a Recurring Cost

1. Open the asset.
2. Find **Recurring Costs**.
3. Select **Add Cost**.
4. Enter the name, amount, frequency, start date, category, and optional notes.
5. Save it.

### Frequencies

Recurring costs can be tracked as:

- One time
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi-annually
- Annually

HomeBase uses the frequency to calculate monthly and annual obligations.

### End Dates

Use an end date when the obligation no longer applies. Expired recurring costs
are excluded from active monthly and annual obligation summaries.

## Fuel Logging

Fuel logging is optimized for quick entry at the pump.

### Quick Log Fuel

Use either:

- The fuel icon in the top bar
- **Log Fuel** on the dashboard
- **Log Fuel** on a vehicle detail page

If HomeBase can identify one active fuel vehicle, it opens the fuel form
directly. If more than one vehicle is available, it asks you to choose one.

### Minimum Fuel Entry

The only required field is:

- Total cost

This is enough to capture fuel spend quickly.

Optional fields:

- Gallons
- Price per gallon
- Odometer
- Station
- Date
- Full tank
- Notes

### MPG

MPG is calculated only when enough information exists. For MPG, log:

- Gallons
- Odometer
- Full tank

HomeBase skips MPG calculations for spend-only logs or partial fillups.

## Cost of Ownership

Every asset detail page includes **Cost of Ownership**.

The main values are:

- **Tracked Total**: purchase price + service spend + fuel spend
- **Annual Run Rate**: last 12 months service + last 12 months fuel + active
  annual recurring costs
- **Monthly Recurring**: current active obligations divided into a monthly view

## Reading the Numbers

- **Tracked Total** is actual known spend.
- **Recurring costs** are projected obligations.
- **Annual Run Rate** estimates current yearly cost based on recent spend and
  active recurring costs.
- **Fuel spend** appears for vehicles only.

## Dashboard Financial Cards

The dashboard shows high-level financial cards and charts. These are meant for
household overview. For the most detailed ownership view, open the asset detail
page.

## Report Charts

The Reports tab includes chart views for household-level trends. Chart PNG
exports are available from the chart panels. Table reports use CSV downloads
instead of image exports.
